Is appropriation actually possible if there's no necessity between a culture and its manifestation? If linguistically signifie incessantly slides off of sifnifiant as Saussure and Pierce (I guess this specific expression by Lacan), then why can't this apply to culture? There's a layer of arbitrary-ness that is prevalent in specific culture. For instance, anime happens to be a Japanese culture, but it isn't necessarily a Japanese culture. When movies like "Pacific Rim" was released, a lot of Japanese people were shocked that westerners were able to recreate the "gigantic humanoid mecha" theme of media (like Evangelion, Ghost in a Shell, Gundam - somethings that were conventionally a Japanese culture), and do it so well. When Hollywood made Kunfu Panda, a lot of Asian critiques said this did the eastern messaging and story telling better than many eastern media nowadays.

This is my point. Just like Signifie and Sifnifiant are arbitrarily connected and conventionally tied but eventually "slides off" each other, the culture we see nowadays are also alike. There's no necessity between what we deem as a culture and its varying manifestation. This is what I mean when I say appropriation is not possible, because nothing about culture is inherently tied to an identity or a cohort - it is eventually and conventionally and arbitrarily so. So, there's no cultural appropriation - there's just well presented interpretation of a culture and a badly presented (or badly conceived and understood) one.

Culture is there to be a common experience, a common ground and context for people. Culture is there to connect and generate common grounds, provide common context for people to relate and connect, not to sever and identify. It is a door, not a wall. It is there to be relished, not to be monopolized, and not to be monetized.

I believe culture is there to be relished. To be a common experience between you and I such that one day there can possibly be "us" in the slim intersection where you and I meet.

If culture is used to sever rather than connect, it is anti-culture.

Even in basic economics, we want the structure and system of K pop to be exported, not the people themselves. there's no necessary reason that this should be true.
