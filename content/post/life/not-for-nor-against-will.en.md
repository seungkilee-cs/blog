## Critique of Structuralist and Post Structuralist Model of Desire and Action

### Bias of Structures and Context and Geworfenheit as a recoil to reductionist rationalism and its horror

- Like the entire Frankfurt school of thought examplified by Adorno and Horkheimer, there are inherent hopelessness and bitter recoiling sentiment against reductionist rationalism. Same philosophical soil exists for Lacanian and even Deleuzian model of desire as an existing structure that human has no control over. While this is true, the Geworfenheit of human condition does not mean that we are mere surface level phenomena of the underlying structures.
- Even post-structuralist thoughts like Deleuze and Gauttari are limited because they inherently misinterpretates the Schopenhaurian wisdom of man being able to "do what he wills but not will what he wills." This observation does not categorically dismiss human ability to overcome desire's grasp on human action

## New Model of Action: Neither For Nor Against wills

- Take your will to do something out of the equation. Do somethinig out of capacity and decision
- Most people do what they want to. This is the problem. Human are malleable but even if we change, we still opt for what we want
- Do what you decide to, within the bounds of what you can. For instance, go to gym at 5:30 in the morning not because you want to or despite not wanting to. Eliminate the desire from the equation. Go to the gym because you decided to go, and you are physically not ill or hurt so you are able to. Sure you are tired and hate it, but how you feel has nothing to do with this anymore in this model.
